Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to start a campaign?

FundHub charges a 5% platform fee on successfully funded campaigns. No upfront costs required.

What happens if my campaign doesn't reach its goal?

Projects using our flexible funding can keep the funds raised. All-or-nothing projects refund backers automatically.

How long should my campaign run?

Optimal campaign duration is 30-60 days. Shorter campaigns create urgency, while longer ones allow more time to build momentum.

Can I edit my campaign after launch?

Yes, you can update your campaign description, add new images, and post updates to keep backers engaged.

When do I receive the funds?

Funds are disbursed within 48 hours of campaign completion, minus the platform fee and payment processing costs.
